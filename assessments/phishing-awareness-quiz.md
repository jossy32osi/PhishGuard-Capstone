# PhishGuard Phishing Awareness Quiz

## Phishing Awareness Knowledge Assessment

### Purpose

This quiz evaluates a participant's understanding of common phishing indicators, safe online behavior, and appropriate responses to suspicious communications.

### Instructions

* Answer all 10 questions.
* Select **one answer** for each question.
* Do not use the answer key until you have completed the quiz.
* Each correct answer is worth **1 point**.
* Maximum score: **10 points**.

---

# Question 1

You receive an unexpected email claiming that your account will be suspended within 30 minutes unless you click a link.

What should you do?

**A.** Click the link immediately.

**B.** Reply and provide your password.

**C.** Stop and independently verify the message.

**D.** Forward it to everyone you know.

# Question 2

**A.** An unexpected request for sensitive information.

**B.** A normal newsletter you intentionally subscribed to.

**C.** A website you accessed directly using a known address.

# Answer Explanations

## Question 1 — C

Urgency is a common phishing tactic. The safest approach is to stop and independently verify the message rather than clicking the provided link.

## Question 2 — A

Unexpected requests for sensitive information are a common warning sign and should be treated cautiously.

## Question 3 — C

Verification codes are sensitive authentication information. They should not be shared simply because someone asks for them.

## Question 4 — B

Unusual financial requests should be independently verified, particularly when urgency is involved.

## Question 5 — C

Unexpected attachments can create security risks. Avoid opening them and follow the appropriate reporting procedure.

## Question 6 — C

A suspicious link should not be trusted simply because the message looks professional or appears to come from a familiar organization.

## Question 7 — C

Verification should be performed through a trusted channel that was obtained independently rather than through information supplied by the suspicious message.

## Question 8 — A

Attackers may use fear, urgency, curiosity, authority, or excitement to encourage people to act without thinking carefully.

## Question 9 — C

If a suspicious link has already been opened, stop interacting with the content and report the incident through the appropriate channel.

## Question 10 — B

The purpose of awareness training is to improve people's ability to recognize threats and respond appropriately.

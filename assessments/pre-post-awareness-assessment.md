# PhishGuard Pre/Post Awareness Assessment

## Measuring Phishing Awareness

### Purpose

This assessment is designed to measure participants' understanding of phishing before and after completing the PhishGuard awareness materials.

The assessment focuses on practical knowledge rather than advanced technical cybersecurity skills.

No passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information should be collected.

# Part 6 — Comparing Pre and Post Results

The assessment should ideally be completed twice:

### Pre-Awareness

Participants complete the assessment **before** reviewing the PhishGuard materials.

### Awareness Training

Participants review:

* Phishing Awareness Guide
* Spot-the-Phish Exercise
* Phishing Response Card
* Phishing Awareness Quiz

### Post-Awareness

Participants complete the assessment again after the awareness session.

# Part 8 — Calculating Improvement

### Example

**Average Pre-Awareness Score:** 31/50

**Average Post-Awareness Score:** 43/50

**Improvement:** 12 points

The project can also report the percentage improvement using:

**Improvement = ((Post Score − Pre Score) / Pre Score) × 100**

Only use actual collected results in the final project report.

Do not present example figures as real participant results.

# Ethical and Privacy Considerations

The assessment should collect only information necessary for evaluating the awareness activity.

Participants should not be asked to provide:

* Passwords
* Verification codes
* Banking information
* Account credentials
* Unnecessary identifying information

Results should be presented in an aggregated or anonymous form whenever possible.

Participation should be voluntary where appropriate, and participants should understand the purpose of the assessment.

---

# Evaluation Objective

The purpose of the pre/post assessment is to determine whether participation in the PhishGuard awareness activity is associated with improved understanding of common phishing indicators and safer responses to suspicious communications.

The results will form part of the evaluation section of the NextGen Capstone project.

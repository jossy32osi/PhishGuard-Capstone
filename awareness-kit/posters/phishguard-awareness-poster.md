# 🚨 THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

## PHISHING CAN LOOK REAL

Phishing messages are designed to trick you into clicking, sharing information, sending money, or giving attackers access to your accounts.

### Learn the warning signs.

---

# 🛑 STOP

Don't rush because a message says:

* "Act now!"
* "Your account will be closed!"
* "Payment required immediately!"
* "Verify your account!"
* "You have won a prize!"

**Urgency is a reason to slow down — not speed up.**

---

# 🔍 CHECK

Ask yourself:

### WHO?

Do I recognize the sender?

### WHAT?

What are they asking me to do?

### WHY?

Why do they need me to do it?

### WAS I EXPECTING THIS?

Unexpected messages deserve extra caution.

---

# 🔗 WATCH THE LINKS

Don't click unexpected links.

A link can lead to a website designed to look like a legitimate service.

Instead:

**Access the official website or application yourself.**

---

# 🔐 PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

Never provide sensitive information simply because a message asks for it.

Be especially careful with:

* Passwords
* Verification codes
* Banking information
* Payment details
* Personal information

---

# 📎 BE CAREFUL WITH ATTACHMENTS

Don't open unexpected attachments just to find out what they contain.

If you're unsure:

**STOP → VERIFY → REPORT**

---

# 🎭 REMEMBER: PEOPLE CAN BE IMPERSONATED

A message may appear to come from:

* Your manager
* A colleague
* Your bank
* Your school
* A delivery company
* Technical support
* A government organization

A familiar name does not automatically make a message trustworthy.

Verify unusual requests through a separate trusted channel.

---

# 🚨 REPORT SUSPICIOUS MESSAGES

If something looks suspicious:

### DON'T CLICK

### DON'T REPLY

### DON'T SHARE INFORMATION

### REPORT IT

Follow your organization's or institution's phishing-reporting procedure.

# 🔍 SPOT THE PHISH

## Phishing Awareness Activity Guide

### Objective

Help participants develop the ability to recognize
common phishing warning signs.

---

## Activity

Participants are shown fictional messages and asked:

> "Is this message legitimate or suspicious?"

They must identify the warning signs that influenced
their decision.

---

## Warning Signs To Look For

Participants should consider:

1. Unexpected requests
2. Urgent or threatening language
3. Suspicious sender addresses
4. Unusual links
5. Requests for passwords or sensitive information
6. Unexpected attachments
7. Spelling or formatting inconsistencies
8. Requests that should be independently verified

---

## Discussion

After each scenario, discuss:

- What made the message suspicious?
- What should the recipient do?
- What should the recipient avoid doing?
- How could the request be verified?

---

## Recommended Response

Use the PhishGuard safety model:

### STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → REPORT

---

## Learning Outcome

Participants should become more confident in:

- Recognizing phishing indicators
- Questioning unexpected requests
- Verifying suspicious communications
- Reporting potential phishing attempts

# PHISHGUARD

## Phishing Response Quick Card

### THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

Received a suspicious email, text, or message?

Follow these four steps:

---

# 1. 🛑 STOP

**Don't act immediately.**

Do not:

* Click unexpected links.
* Open unexpected attachments.
* Reply to the message.
* Send money.
* Share passwords.
* Share verification codes.
* Provide sensitive information.

Take a moment to think before doing anything.

---

# 2. 🔍 CHECK

Ask yourself:

### WHO sent it?

Does the sender address, phone number, or account look correct?

### WHAT are they asking for?

Are they requesting:

* Passwords?
* Verification codes?
* Money?
* Personal information?
* Account access?

### WHY is it urgent?

Is the message trying to make you panic, rush, or act without thinking?

### WAS I expecting this?

If you weren't expecting the message, be extra cautious.

---

# 3. ✅ VERIFY

**Never trust the message alone.**

If the message claims to come from a bank, company, school, colleague, delivery service, or other organization:

* Go to the official website yourself.
* Use an official application.
* Contact the organization using a trusted phone number.
* Speak to the person through a separate trusted communication channel.

**Do not use contact information or links provided by a suspicious message to verify the message.**

---

# 4. 🚨 REPORT

If you believe a message is phishing:

**Report it using the appropriate reporting process.**

Depending on where you received it, this may mean reporting it to:

* Your organization's IT/security team
* Your school or institution
* The relevant service provider
* The appropriate reporting authority

Follow your organization's procedures for handling suspicious messages.

---

# ⚠️ COMMON PHISHING WARNING SIGNS

Watch for:

* Unexpected messages
* Urgent deadlines
* Threats or fear
* Suspicious links
* Unexpected attachments
* Requests for passwords
* Requests for verification codes
* Requests for money
* Unusual payment instructions
* Fake account warnings
* Impersonation of trusted people or organizations
* Requests that seem unusual or out of character

---

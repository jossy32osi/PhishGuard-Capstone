RESPONSE GUIDE

What To Do When You Suspect Phishing

Don't panic and don't rush. Follow these four simple steps.

1

STOP

Stop before clicking links, opening attachments, replying, or providing personal information.

2

CHECK

Look carefully at the sender, message, links, spelling, requests, and urgency.

3

VERIFY

Confirm the request through a trusted channel. Do not rely only on contact information contained in the suspicious message.

4

REPORT

Report suspicious messages using your organization's approved reporting process.

⚠️ Remember

Legitimate organizations should not pressure you into making unsafe decisions. When something feels unusual, pause and verify it.

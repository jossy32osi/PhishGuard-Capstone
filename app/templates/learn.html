PHISHING AWARENESS

Learn to Recognize Phishing

Understanding the warning signs is one of the best ways to protect yourself from phishing attacks.

01

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering in which an attacker attempts to trick someone into revealing information, clicking a harmful link, opening an unsafe attachment, or taking another unsafe action.

02

Watch for Urgency

Phishing messages often create pressure by claiming that something must be done immediately.

⚠️ Be cautious when a message says things like: "Act now", "Your account will be suspended", or "Immediate action required."
03

Check the Sender

Don't assume that a message is legitimate simply because it contains a familiar company name or logo.

🔎 Carefully check the sender's address and look for unusual domains, spelling, or unexpected requests.
04

Be Careful With Links

Unexpected links can lead to fake websites designed to collect information.

🔗 Don't click suspicious links. Verify the request through a trusted method instead.
05

Protect Sensitive Information

Be cautious when a message unexpectedly asks for passwords, verification codes, financial information, or other sensitive details.

🔐 Never provide sensitive information simply because an unexpected message asks for it.
06

Verify Before Acting

If a request seems unusual, independently verify it using a trusted website, phone number, or known contact.

✓ STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → REPORT

Remember

Phishing relies on urgency, trust, fear, curiosity, and authority to influence decisions.

When in doubt, don't click.
